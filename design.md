# Noven Design Direction

## Brand Feel
Minimal, premium, intelligent, trustworthy.

## Product
AI Biology Examiner Assistant for Edexcel IGCSE and A-Level students.

## Design Principles
- Simple over complicated
- Professional over playful
- Clear over crowded
- Modern over traditional

## Target Users
Students preparing for high-stakes Biology exams.

## Core Emotion
"I finally have a tool that understands exactly how exams are marked."
