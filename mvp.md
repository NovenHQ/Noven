# Noven MVP

## Product:
AI Biology Examiner Assistant

## Target:
Edexcel IGCSE and A-Level Biology students.

## Core Feature:
Students submit a Biology exam question and their answer. The AI evaluates it using examiner-style feedback.

## User Journey:
1. Student enters question.
2. Student enters answer.
3. AI analyzes response.
4. Student receives marks, mistakes, and improved answer.
