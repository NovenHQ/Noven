# Product Idea

## Problem:
Many Edexcel IGCSE and A-Level Biology students understand the content but lose marks because their answers do not match examiner expectations and mark scheme requirements.

## Target User:
Edexcel IGCSE Biology and A-Level Biology students preparing for exams.

## Solution:
An AI-powered Biology exam assistant that analyzes student answers, compares them with mark schemes, identifies missing keywords, and gives actionable feedback.

## Why Now:
Modern AI can provide instant personalized feedback and help students practice exam technique at a scale that traditional tutoring cannot.

## First Version:
A simple web app where students paste a Biology exam question and their answer, then receive AI-powered marking feedback based on Edexcel requirements.
